ReSeed Quest — for parents and teachers

25 short browser games for ages 8–12 that follow the real ReSeed program step by step: satellites find lost forest, a neural network checks it, a greenness score (NDVI) decides, and seed drones plant it back. Along the way children practise typing and meet the computer-science ideas the program really uses. They are on missions, not in lessons; every real term they meet becomes a collectible Field Card.

⏱️ Short and saved

Each level takes 1–3 minutes, each game 3–5 levels. Stars and cards are saved in this browser. After about 20 minutes, Sprout suggests a stretch break.

🔒 Private

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no network calls while playing. The only thing stored is progress and an optional nickname, in this browser's local storage. Settings → "Start over" erases it.

🧭 Honest

Numbers presented as ReSeed's come from the ReSeed code and its real results on 13 places. Where a game simplifies, the Field Card says "game version". Command-line, installer and cloud screens are simulations: nothing is installed.

Playing

  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a computer or a tablet in landscape. Typing games need a keyboard.
  • Online: khamydoan.github.io/reseed_games. Offline: download the folder and double-click index.html.
  • Voices: recorded with an AI voice (gpt-audio-1.5); lines without a recording use the computer's own voice if "read aloud" is on.
  • Characters (Sprout, Koa, Tuki, Professor Hoot, Pix, Mo and the Glitch Bugs) are original to this project.
